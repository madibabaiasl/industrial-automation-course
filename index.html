MENG 4450

PLC & Robotics

Learn the core lifecycle of industrial automation: sensors collect data, controllers make decisions, and actuators do the work. In this course, you’ll get hands-on experience building, operating, and troubleshooting real hardware systems so you can help keep modern production lines running.

The premise

Master universal principles. Adapt to any machine.

Understand the principles that govern how automated systems sense, decide, and act. With that foundation, you can approach unfamiliar machines, control systems, and robotic equipment with confidence.

How the course runs

Read, simulate, build

01 · LEARN

Principle first

A short lesson introduces the principle one step at a time, with practice questions that are ungraded, unlimited, and worked through on submission.

02 · SIMULATE

Rehearse the logic

You write and run your own control logic against an emulated controller and a plant model, change parameters, and debug a live system where a mistake costs nothing.

03 · BUILD

Apply on the bench

The same principle, a different task, on the controller benches and the robot cells. Success is transfer, and no two benches are wired the same way.
