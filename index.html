Think out loud.

This site holds the interactive activities we run during class meetings. Each one is built for two people sharing one screen: you talk first, commit to an answer, and see the reasoning the moment you do. The reading gives you the principle, the bench gives you the hardware, and this is where the two meet.

How to use

How these activities work

In class

One device between two students

Every activity is built for pairs sharing a screen, because the argument between the two of you is where the learning happens. Everything runs inside the page: open it and play, and when the tab closes, what it showed you is gone.

On any screen

Phones, laptops, the bench machines

Every interaction is a single tap, so the smallest phone works as well as a laptop or a bench machine. Open the link and you are in, and the whole room is playing within seconds.

What you get

Feedback, on the spot

Principle comes from the assigned reading, and grades live on Canvas. This site gives you immediate feedback while there is still time to act on it: every answer shows its reasoning, and syllabus questions quote the sentence they came from. Scores stay between the two of you playing.

Design rule

The answer is in the room

Wherever an activity can, it turns on something you read off the equipment in front of you: this bench, this pendant, this nameplate. Answering means standing in the room with the machine.
