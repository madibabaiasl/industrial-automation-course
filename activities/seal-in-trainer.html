Free run: hold a button to press it, release to let go. Switch to single scan to step the scan by hand, where the buttons latch instead.

Two coils, one variable. Rung 1 still looks satisfied while you hold Start, yet Motor_Run reads 0 and the seal never establishes. Both rungs execute every scan, top to bottom, and the last write to a variable is the one that stands. Rung 2 writes 0 after rung 1 wrote 1.
