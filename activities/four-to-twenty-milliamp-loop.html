MENG 4450, Lesson 3.2. Assigned reading: Kuphaldt, Lessons in Industrial Instrumentation v2.30, section 13.1 pages 863 to 866, section 13.2 pages 867 to 868, section 13.2.6 pages 880 to 882, sections 13.4 and 13.5 pages 891 to 894, and section 12.2.2 pages 786 to 787.

Model. The transmitter is taken as ideal, so I = 4 + 16(level/100) mA. Conductor resistance is 3.3 Ω per 100 m per conductor, which is close to 18 AWG copper. In the voltage comparison the sender delivers 1 to 5 V into the 250 Ω receiver and the conductor resistance appears in series with it. Converter counts follow the worked example on page 880, where a 16 bit card returns 3277 counts at 4 mA and 16384 counts at 20 mA, and the other resolutions are scaled from that. The moving dashes indicate the presence and magnitude of current and are not to any time scale.
