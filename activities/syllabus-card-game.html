Day one · Card game · Play in pairs

Read the Syllabus

Three rounds of cards. Where the marks go, how each piece of work is graded, and what actually happens in eight situations you will meet this term.

Before you start

One device between two of you.

Talk before you tap. If the two of you disagree, that disagreement is the point of the exercise, and whichever of you is wrong will remember this longer than reading the document would have made you.

Twenty-one cards in three rounds. Nothing is recorded and nothing is submitted. The score is only so you know whether you have actually read it.

How this runs in class

Twelve to fifteen minutes. Pairs, one device. Walk the room while they play; the arguments you overhear tell you which policy is unclear, and that is worth more than the scores.

  • Round 1 is the weight table. Most pairs put the capstone above the laboratory work and are surprised they are equal.
  • Round 2 is the completion against quality distinction. This is the one students get wrong all term if it is not fixed on day one.
  • Round 3 deals eight situation cards from a pack of twelve, so a second play is not the same game.

Close by asking which card caused the longest argument, and answer that one out loud.
