What will go here

  • 01The hazards, concretelyComposite speed up to 6.9 m/s in a 350 mm envelope, a vertical axis that drops when its brake releases, pinch points, stored pneumatic energy.
  • 02Safeguarded space and the risk assessment that defines itISO 10218-1:2025 and ISO 10218-2:2025, third editions.
  • 03Emergency stopISO 13850:2015. Independent of the protective stop, and hardwired.
  • 04The three-position enabling deviceIEC 60947-5-8:2020, cited on the pendant's own label. Releasing from the squeezed position must not re-enable motion.
  • 05Reduced speed in manual mode250 mm/s, enforced whenever a person may be inside the safeguarded space.
  • 06What is out of scope, and why that mattersNothing you build in this course is safety-rated, and you should know that.
  • 07The second-person ruleOne student on the pendant, one outside at the emergency stop doing nothing else.
